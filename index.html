Our flavours

Something for every sweet tooth.

From the Classic that started it all to Biscoff, Pistachio and Creme Brulee. Every cake is prepared by hand, and flavours rotate weekly in the shop.

  • Classic

    Laila’s melt-in-your-mouth creamy cheesecake, the one that started it all. Great on its own, even better with your favourite topping.

  • Strawberry

    Homemade, sweet strawberry sauce over our classic creamy cheesecake and graham crust.

  • Lemon

    Just the right amount of tart. Infused with hand-squeezed and zested lemon on a graham crust, topped with homemade lemon curd.

  • Blueberry

    Homemade, mouth-watering blueberry sauce over our classic creamy cheesecake and cinnamon graham crust.

  • Cinnamon Roll

    Creamy cheesecake loaded with homemade cinnamon roll filling, topped with homemade cream cheese frosting and salted caramel crisps!

  • Cookies & Cream

    Creamy cheesecake loaded with Oreo cookies on a chocolate cookie crust, topped with homemade whipped cream.

  • Peanut Butter

    Loaded with peanut buttery goodness! Just the perfect amount of peanut butter, chocolate ganache and a swirl of smooth peanut butter.

  • Chocolate

    Chocolate lovers rejoice! Laila’s infamously creamy chocolate cheesecake topped with chocolate ganache.

  • Biscoff Cookie Butter

    Creamy cheesecake infused with Biscoff cookie butter, topped with more cookie butter, whipped cream and Lotus cookies!

  • Cherry

    Our cheesecake topped with our freshly made sweet dark cherry sauce!

  • Salted Caramel

    Creamy vanilla cheesecake topped with caramel popcorn and homemade salted caramel sauce.

  • Chocolate Hazelnut

    Chocolate hazelnut spread and Laila’s classic cheesecake over a chocolate cookie crust, finished with homemade whipped chocolate ganache.

  • Creme Brulee

    A layer of caramelized sugar over creamy cheesecake, with a melt-in-your-mouth vanilla cookie crust underneath.

  • Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough

    Loaded with chunks of homemade cookie dough over a chocolate cookie crust, topped with homemade chocolate chip cookies and a chocolate drizzle.

  • Caramel Chocolate Pecan

    Our turtles-inspired cheesecake with in-house chocolate ganache and caramel sauce, topped with premium pecans!

  • Pistachio

    Pistachios all around! A graham crust under pistachio cheesecake, topped with house-made pistachio cream, crushed pistachios and whipped cream.

  • Raspberry White Chocolate

    Raspberries, white chocolate ganache and Laila’s signature cheesecake? Yes, please!

  • Brownie

    Cheesecake and brownies unite! A chocolate cookie crust, our vanilla cheesecake, and a topping of chocolate brownies.

Our story

Creating happiness with every bite.

Here at Laila’s Cheesecake Co., our goal is to create happiness with every bite. We bake with real ingredients and believe cheesecake should never be a source of anything except pure bliss.

We bake every cheesecake, oh so gently, so it’s like velvet on your tongue.

Meet Laila

Is there really a baker named Laila?

Yes! That’s me!

It all began with my pregnancy cravings, which sparked the desire to bake a delicious and flavourful cheesecake. With tremendous love and support from family and friends, Laila’s Cheesecake Co. was born.

Fast forward to today, I still take pride in sourcing the best ingredients and preparing each cake by hand.

Laila’s Cheesecake Co.

They say the proof is in the pudding. At Laila’s, it’s in the cheese!
